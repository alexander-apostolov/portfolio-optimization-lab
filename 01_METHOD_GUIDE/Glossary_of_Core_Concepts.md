# Glossary of Core Concepts

**Admissible**: passes required budget and constraint feasibility thresholds and deterministic staging.
**Budget profile**: available budget by period.
**Constraint capacity profile**: available scarce portfolio resource by period.
**Cost of time / value-of-time profile**: project-specific timing-value logic relative to a reference date, not merely financial discounting.
**P85 profit**: primary stochastic profit percentile.
**Structural repair**: rule-based repair of dependencies; for aOctane, selection can differ from Octane only through this repair.
**WIP**: active projects in a period.
**Management reserve**: unused budget envelope retained for black swans, overruns, scope changes, or reallocation.
